========================================================================
SENTINEL-X ANDROID APPLICATION — INSTALLATION GUIDE
========================================================================

Follow these simple steps to install SENTINEL-X on any Android phone
or tablet:

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: EXTRACT THE ZIP FILE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Extract "SENTINEL-X-Android.zip" on your Android device (using Files by
Google, Samsung My Files, or any file manager).

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: TAP AND OPEN THE APK
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Tap on "SENTINEL-X.apk" located inside the extracted folder.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: ALLOW INSTALLATION FROM THIS SOURCE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
If your Android phone shows:
"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source"

1. Tap "Settings" on the prompt.
2. Toggle ON "Allow from this source".
3. Tap the Back button to return to the installer.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4: COMPLETE INSTALLATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Tap "Install". The installation will complete in a few seconds.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5: LAUNCH SENTINEL-X
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Tap "Open", or tap the new "SENTINEL-X" icon on your phone's home screen
or app drawer.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 6: PERMISSION PROMPTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
When prompted:
1. Location: Choose "While using the app" (allows GPS emergency pinpoint).
2. Microphone: Choose "While using the app" (allows Voice Emergency intake).

You are now ready to use SENTINEL-X Dual Emergency Intelligence!
