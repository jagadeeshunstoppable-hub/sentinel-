========================================================================
SENTINEL-X ANDROID APPLICATION — USER GUIDE
========================================================================

SENTINEL-X provides two complete operational platforms in a single
unified Android application:

========================================================================
PLATFORM 1: CIVILIAN EMERGENCY RESPONSE & DISPATCH (108 / 112 TN)
========================================================
Designed for citizens, responders, and 108 CAD dispatchers.

MODES:
1. CITIZEN MODE:
   - 🚨 1-Tap SOS: Instant emergency help alert with sound and GPS tag.
   - 🎙️ Speak Emergency: Voice intake supporting Tamil, English, and Hindi.
   - ⌨️ Type Emergency: Fast structured text distress form with casualty count.
   - 📍 Location Pinpoint: Real GPS coordinates and landmark verification.
   - 🚑 Live Tracking: Real-time ambulance ETA countdown, route, and hospital.

2. DISPATCHER CONSOLE:
   - Complete 3-Zone CAD console with triage assessments, vehicle dispatch,
     reroute simulation, trauma vitals, and cross-agency coordination.

3. EASY / ELDERLY MODE:
   - Clean, uncluttered screen with high-contrast buttons (>48px) and voice-first
     assistance for elderly or low-literacy citizens.

========================================================================
PLATFORM 2: INDUSTRIAL SAFETY & 3D DIGITAL TWIN COMMAND CENTER (SCADA)
========================================================================
Designed for industrial plant safety officers, engineers, and command staff.

FEATURES:
- 3D Interactive Spatial Digital Twin powered by Three.js WebGL.
- Real-time hazard raycasting, gas plume dispersion, and risk heatmaps.
- MQTT sensor bus telemetry with Byzantine fault tolerance.
- Worker evacuation pathfinding and hardware readiness monitoring.
- Photo-based library digital twin with multi-angle facility inspection.

========================================================================
SWITCHING BETWEEN PLATFORMS
========================================================================
- On the main home screen, tap either "🚨 CIVILIAN (108/112)" or "🏭 INDUSTRIAL TWIN".
- Inside Civilian mode, tap "🏭 Industrial Safety →" in the header.
- Inside Industrial mode, tap "Civilian Emergency (108/112) →" at the top.
- Pressing Android Hardware Back returns to previous views and closes modals.
